# Level 25 (start-overlap issue)
The player spawns overlapping the gravity block at (-16,16). If that overlap fires collisionStart on step 0 (as the sim does by default), gravity flips up and the best route is 106 frames (exhaustive-ish search):
`loadInputs(["j0",14,"j",49,"j",11,"j",15,"j"])`
If the flip does NOT fire on step 0 (matches the 45-frame route seen in game), the sim (env NOGRAV0=1 / NOPAIR0=1 in work/L25/sim) gives:
- 44 frames, valid under both no-flip models: `loadInputs(["j",6,"j",2,"j",1,"j",3,"j",2,"j"])`
- 45 frames with j0 (NOGRAV0 only): `loadInputs(["j0",4,"j",3,"j",1,"j",2,"j",2,"j"])`
