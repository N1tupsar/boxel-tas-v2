# Level 23 (single agent)
Result: 349 frames (5.82 s); target 6 s (360). Met.
Route: leave the start frame at its top-right, enter the tower (the hook formed by the x=-80 wall and the spiky plate) from below, climb to the right-hand column, corner-kick off the underside of the x=104 wall to get vx -4.5 at y~570, fly left over the plate, fall down the left side and jump once at the bottom to pop under the big wall into the finish.
Method that worked: (1) grid beam with LA=5 and seeded score noise (391); (2) a custom beam (vl7.js) that fixes the first ticks, steers to the plate's left end with a target leftward speed and, whenever a state is in the descent window, brute-forces the single final jump time (found 366, 364, 349).
What did not work: Euclid-waypoint chains, plain suffix sweeps, refine.
