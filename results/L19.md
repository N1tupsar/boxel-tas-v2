# Level 19 (agent A, round 1)
Layout: start at bottom of a narrow vertical corridor (zigzag bumps on both walls) climbing to a top corridor with 2 resize sensors, going right then down a chute to a bowl; huge finish (240x190) bottom right, far below/right of the bowl.
Ideas: wall-jump chain via the bumps, skip resize (or use), ceiling surf along the top corridor, chute rebound, bowl launch right/up to land in the huge finish, j0, direct launch from the corridor top over to finish, tunnelling thin walls, corner kicks on bumps.
