# Level 17 (excluded originally: loose bounce pads)
In-game symptom (user): the run reaches the end every time but the bounce-pad field at the finish behaves differently each attempt.
Cause (found from game_source_excerpts.js:359): a LOOSE pad hit by the cube is launched with `e *= -1 * app.interval.speed`, a frame-time dependent value, so the pad field diverges every run. The simulator assumes 1. In the sim the route below only finishes at speed exactly 1 (0.9/1.1 -> no finish).
Fix: deterministic_patch.js now pins `app.interval.speed` to `window.__ISPEED` (default 1) and logs the original value (`[det] app.interval.speed was ...`).
First route (beam W300 TIME=4): 385 frames. Replays at 385 for alpha 0.3/0.7, SNAP/LOADRESET/STARTJUMP off. Not yet optimised, not yet tested in game.

## First in-game test (user), analysed from __log
- Player path matches the sim exactly (<1 px) for the first ~330 ticks.
- At ~tick 331 (x~1707) the real cube hits something and loses speed (vx 8.1 -> 5.0); the sim has nothing there. It then reaches the shaft and rises to game y ~17, about 15 px below the finish (y 32..48), i.e. "doesn't quite reach the finish".
- app.interval.speed was already 1, so it is not the cause.
- Loose pads fall and pile up in the shaft for 330 ticks (18 pads + 2 cubes colliding) - a chaotic pile whose outcome depends on Matter's body/pair order. Suspect: world body order or solver order differs between game and sim.
- Diagnostics added to the patch: `__order` (world body order at level start) and `__loose` (loose body positions at ticks 1,30,100,200,300,330). Sim reference: results/L17_sim_loose.json.
