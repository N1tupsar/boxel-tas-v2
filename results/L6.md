## Route (completely different from the old loop route)
1. Roll down the start ramp (no early pumping helps; beams with angle-aware keys confirm x(t) is maximal for the plain descent), jump at tick 85 on the bowl floor and fly at vx 7.53 over the upturn.
2. Touch the 8-speed pad at (568,-136) flatly (vx becomes 7.98), keep flying right just above the pad block.
3. Fly under the descending-right "valley" ceiling pieces at x 660..830 (y -89..-144) and do a **ceiling bonk chain**: 9 jumps in about 20 ticks. Each jump hits the underside of a descending-right slope, whose normal points down-left, so the jump turns into leftward speed. On a slope of angle th the iteration is s' = cos(th)^2 s - 6.67 sin(th) (asymptote vx = -6.67 cot(th); the 30 deg piece gives -11.5). The cube leaves the last piece at x about 650 with vx -11.0.
4. Coast left over the pad block, the bowl and the start ramp at vx -11.02 (one more jump around tick 184 keeps the height inside the finish window), touch the finish at x about -72. The finish box is only 72 px from the start, but nothing pushes the cube left until the chain.

## Why the old searches never moved left
No leftward force exists; leftward speed only comes from (a) landing on up-left-normal slopes (bowl upturn), (b) bonks into undersides of descending-right slopes, (c) tilted-corner kicks (a spinning cube hitting a block corner converts vertical speed into sideways speed). Grid beams that use a distance-to-finish score never visit the right region and then return; a score based on mechanical energy H = y + v^2/(2g) plus `ALPHA*x + BETA*vx` (maximise energy while pushing leftward) found the first left-going routes (306 frames), and then an angle-aware dedupe key (cube angle mod 90 deg and angular velocity in the key) was essential to get from 237 down to 231.

## Methods / tools (all in work/L6/sim)
- `fast.js`: numeric snapshot/restore of the player body, its hitbox part and the pair cache (about 90 us per save+load+step vs 3 ms cloneSim). Needed pair vertices mapped back to live vertices and the `collisionFilter.category` restored (kill zeroes it).
- `fb.js`: fast beam (env ALPHA BETA GAMMA TW CMIN NOISE SEED KA KQ PREJ T0). `KA` (angle bins) is the key setting.
- `exh.js`, `exh2.js`, `sweep.js`, `sweep2.js`: exhaustive chain enumeration + analytic/real flight evaluation.
- `sa.js`: annealing. `hb.js`: first energy beam (clone based).
